# The builder's LIRA-relaxed entries (spec docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-builder-database-design.md §5.1):
# molecules within H–Ar that PubChem has no 3D shape for. The generator
#   cargo test -p app --release --bin app -- worker::tests::generate_lira_shapes --ignored --nocapture
# relaxes each one with the app's own Relax (GPU, LSDA, Accurate, ionic accepted, automatic multiplicity) and writes
# it into molecules.txt as a `source lira` row; an entry PubChem does have a 3D shape for is skipped (PubChem's wins).
#
# One entry a line, five fields split by `|`:
#   name | synonyms (comma-separated, may be empty) | atoms | bonds | multiplicity
# atoms: element symbols in order (numbered from 0), a trailing + or - for a formal charge of ±1;
# bonds: a-b single, a=b double, a#b triple;
# multiplicity: `auto` (the app's automatic multiplicity: paired, one unpaired electron for an odd count).
# The formal charges are the builder's (crates/app/src/builder/chem.rs): where a drawing with no valence
# warning exists it is the one listed (BH4- on B, the dative N+–B- of ammonia borane, the bridging Cl+ of
# Al2Cl6); a bridging H (B2H6, H3+) has no warning-free form and is drawn plain.
#
# The relaxed shapes are this solver's minima at those defaults, not experimental geometries: bonds to Na, Mg and Be come
# out 2–10 % long against experiment (Al has no sourced gas-phase value to compare): Li, Be, Na, Mg and Al are
# all-electron with smeared nuclei under `accepted` at h = σ = 0.15.
# Measured and explained in crates/validate/references.md, "Builder LIRA shapes: all-electron light metals relax long".
