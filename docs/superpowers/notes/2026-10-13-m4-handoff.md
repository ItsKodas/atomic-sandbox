## Honest limits (say them in the UI and README)

- **Water at h = 0.25 is not water.** The `h2o` preset (physical geometry) sits 0.160 Ha (4.4 eV) above the σ = 0.25 energy minimum, which is near-linear with O–H ≈ 2.34 Bohr; Play lets it fall toward that minimum and heat to thousands of kelvin. Resolution limit of the smeared all-electron oxygen; pseudopotentials or a finer grid are the fix. Measured at the preset's 57³ (the 33³ figures in older notes carried a box-too-small warning).
- **Warm-SCF stall on fast-changing density:** 57³ MD from the h2o preset stalls on step 2 (Δρ 8.5e-5 after 200 iterations, with or without extrapolation/thermostat, at tol 1e-5 and 1e-6) while cold solves converge. R15 (final fix wave) retries once cold; the trigger (warm + extrapolated DIIS stagnation near a fast-changing density / level crossing) is unresolved — solver item for M5/M6.
- **Interactive drags are ~1e13× faster than the physics.** The spring is critically damped and force-capped at 0.1 Ha/Bohr; drags during play are slow (~0.2 Bohr per wall second at 3 fs/s) and the injected work is shown. A vigorous uncapped drag injected 1.1 Ha and sent H₂ out of the box — now impossible (cap + cell guard).
- **Frame rate:** 49³ H₂ ≈ 10 steps/s ≈ 2.5–3.5 fs/s. "Watch it move" is a 4-second vibration. This is why M5 is the surrogate provider.
- **Frozen rotations:** a molecule started from rest has L = 0 exactly and CSVR preserves it; the driver's Maxwell–Boltzmann start on enable avoids the (3N−3)/(3N−6) vibrational overheating; DOF stays 3N−3 because the grid breaks rotational invariance.
- **Thermostat + pins:** DOF counts pinned nuclei (edge case, documented).
- **Gravity hook:** g = 9.8 m/s² ≈ 1e-22 Bohr/a.u.² — invisible by design; the thermostat preserves COM motion (R10), so a thermostatted system under the field falls at the NVE pace.
