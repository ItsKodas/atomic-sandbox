Atomic Sandbox UI

The common actions move to a bottom toolbar; set-and-forget settings move into menus; everything you read moves into a tabbed dock on the right. Every control and readout of today's side panel has a place here, and the appendix maps each one, 148 in total.

Bottom toolbar: what your hands doTime, speed, dt, tool mode, element, selection actions and the solver with its trust light.
Menus: what you set onceFile, Scene (electrons, grid), View (density, display aids), Physics (thermostat, field, COM), Help.
Dock: what you readReadouts, Graph, Model, Selection and Log tabs. Collapses to an icon strip whose dots still show drift, trust and errors.
Physics caveats stay visibleShort lines stay in place, the long text sits behind i tooltips and collapsible sections, and the full set is under Help.

Interaction notes

  • Space plays and pauses (no key repeat), as today. Delete removes the selected nucleus, also while playing. Esc deselects new; the app has no Esc binding today.
  • Drag. Moves arrive at the window's frame rate (240 Hz on the RX 7900 XTX test machine). Paused, the nucleus goes where you drop it; playing, it is pulled by a critically damped spring capped at 0.1 Ha/Bohr, drawn as the orange ring and tether. Drag moves never abort the solve in flight, and the worker stays paced under a flood of them (worker test: one move every ~4 ms for 2 s, sim rate 15.5 fs/s against 15.6 paced). Targets and placements stay at least 3 grid steps inside the cell.
  • Watchable sets the steps per tick so one ~10 fs H–X vibration takes about 0.5 s of wall time (≈ 20 fs/s target) at the current dt and measured cost per step. Above ~30 fs/s a bond vibration is faster than the frame rate: you see a shimmer, not a swing. Speed never changes dt (rule 8).
  • Picking an element chip switches the tool to Place new; today the element combo and the tool radio are separate.
  • Two-click confirms stay inline (Forget model, Start anyway above 2 min), with no modal dialogs.
  • Ctrl K new searches every control by name, including the ones in menus and collapsibles.
  • Scene edits (charge, multiplicity, σ, grid, preset, load) still pause the run and restart its time. The status chip says so, and the change goes to the Log.
