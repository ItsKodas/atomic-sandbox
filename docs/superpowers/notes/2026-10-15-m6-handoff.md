## Honest limits

- **H₂ dissociation stays spin-symmetric.** `multiplicity: None` gives n↑ = n↓ and the cold start ρ↑ = ρ↓, so
  a stretched H₂ converges to the spin-symmetric solution: q rises again past 2.5 Bohr and the pair is still
  "bonded" at 5 Bohr (the floor drops it between 5 and 6), and the energy is ≈ +0.05 Ha too high at
  dissociation. A solver follow-up: for an even-N lowest multiplicity also try a spin-asymmetric start and
  keep the lower energy. README states it.
- **Shell inversion at h = 0.25.** The σ = h smeared nucleus puts oxygen's (and neon's) 2s above its 2p
  (the orbital-viewer Task 0 spike on branch `orbital-viewer`, commit 62ed674, `docs/superpowers/notes/2026-10-15-orbital-viewer-task0-spike.md` there):
  the O singlet is 1s² 2p⁶. The bond detector's O reference is that singlet (the triplet and quintet stop at
  the 200-iteration cap on the GPU, Task 3); scaling the O table by ±10 % moves no verdict (Task 7), but
  every O-containing number (ρ_b, q) is a smeared-atom number. Topology is expected right; values are not
  physical.
- **Heavy atoms at h = 0.25** are smeared: X–H bonds come through Route D, water relaxes near-linear.
- **HeH⁺ and LiH** come out bonded; the accumulation test is a covalent criterion and the spec left ionic and
  closed-shell bonds open (§3.6). No charge-dependent promolecule rescaling.
- **He₂'s depletion** (q 0.971) is resolved against He₂'s own noise, not against the gridded fixture's 3.4 %.
- **FIRE is not faster than the quench on small molecules** (H₃⁺: 25 vs 16 evaluations).
- **The bond overlay is a line probe**, not QTAIM: no bond paths or basins; chords are chords.
- **The periodic table** greys Z > 10 ("needs pseudopotentials (planned)"); names are listed to Z = 54,
  beyond that the symbol and Z; isotopes exist for H–Ne only (the user's request to extend the isotope table to
  every element is recorded; Task 10 was told not to change `units::isotopes`).
- **UI not exercised in a window** during Task 10 (headless shots and egui pointer tests only).
