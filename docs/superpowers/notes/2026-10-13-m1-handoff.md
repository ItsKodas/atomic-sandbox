## Notes for the GPU implementer (M3)

- Grid layout `idx = i + n0*(j + n1*k)`, uniform h, origin −(n−1)h/2. Dirichlet ghosts are zero for ψ and for the Poisson interior operator; the boundary enters as a separate source term L_bc from the l ≤ 6 solid-harmonic expansion (49 moments, each an inner product against precomputed R_lm). Pad GPU buffers by 4 ghost cells and use one unbranched stencil kernel.
- Reductions are the contract: `Grid::dot`/`integrate` are Neumaier in f64. GPU: two-level workgroup reduction with f32 partials, f64 accumulation of partials on the CPU. Everything feeding energies, DIIS and LOBPCG Gram matrices, and multipole moments goes through this.
- Poisson: CPU CG uses a 1e-10 relative residual (f32 floors near 1e-6); iterations scale ∝ n (87 → 166 for 25³ → 49³) so multigrid is needed at 101³+; Jacobi is a no-op on a uniform grid; warm-starting V_H cuts iterations > 4×.
- Preconditioner: `JACOBI_SWEEPS = 4` damped Jacobi on (H − λ)w = r, ω = 4/(3ρ) = 0.584 from the stencil symbol, D = max(diag(H) − λ, 0.1); one sweep is a scalar and does nothing; ω > 0.83 makes T indefinite at s = 4.
- LOBPCG: full MGS of [X, W, P] each iteration; Rayleigh–Ritz (≤ 3k × 3k) in f64 on CPU; orthogonalised-P variant.
- DIIS: Gram over stacked spin residuals, scaled by max diagonal, +1e-12 relative regularisation (raise to ~1e-6 in f32), oldest entry dropped on LU failure down to linear mixing.
- Energy is evaluated from ρ_out with one extra Poisson solve; force accuracy is bounded by `eig_tol`, not `tol`.
- Cost baseline: ~3 s per DIIS step at 81³ on one CPU thread, 10–18 steps cold, 6 warm.
