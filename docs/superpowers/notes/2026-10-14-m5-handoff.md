- `surrogate` crate (pure f64, depends on `units` only): Chebyshev radial × cosine cutoff, real solid harmonics (Racah, l ≤ 4) with analytic gradients; species-channelled body-2 and body-3 descriptors (the real-harmonic invariant Σ_m A·A; a total-degree cap, default D = 4; body-3 default on), forces as exact gradients (ΣF, torque to 1e-12; FD 1e-8); a binding-energy fit against solver-computed isolated-atom references (lowest-energy multiplicity); scale-only standardisation; a two-noise ridge posterior with evidence-estimated (λ, s_E, s_F) on Q′ class representatives (hetero-pair body-2 columns are tied — only their sums are identifiable); Bayesian predictive variance (force std = expected ‖δF‖, energy std total); a bit-exact `.model` sidecar (v2, with samples, fit report, quarantine codes, per-sample face density).
- `sim::SurrogateKs`: a `ForceProvider` wrapping the DFT teacher. Decision list: no model → provenance/species/charge mismatch → untrusted (trust = n_samples ≥ Q′, residual dofs ≥ 30, max training residual within the policy thresholds) → predict → OOD envelope (training-spread z-score) → variance (per-component force std vs max(2e-3, 10 %|F|) capped 1e-2; epistemic energy std per atom ≤ 1e-3) → near-quarantine fence → background audit every 2000 answered steps. Teaching: box-affected answers (face density > 2e-5·(h/0.25)²) are used but never trained on; thinning of fallback samples (variance-rule fallbacks exempt); quarantine of samples the model cannot represent after a degree-cap-6 retry, capped at 10 % of the store, with release/supersede when fresh answers agree, and three consistent disagreeing answers inside a fence keeping one sample for the next refit to judge; refits rate-limited (0.5 s / 25 samples) with every outcome visible; one-surface energy accounting (model energy on fallback steps, force-mismatch work and refit jumps booked into W).
- `sim::sampling`: scene-derived sampling pass (perturbations, hot CSVR burst, pair scans at 0.7–1.8× of the current separations — never a radius table), isolated-atom references per species (Hund's rule by energy scan), resumable one solve per tick slice with ETA and a confirmation above 2 min.
- Viewer: solver radio CPU / GPU / **estimated**; sampling panel; readouts (source badge, uncertainty vs threshold, trusted + reason, samples/epoch/rmse, fallback fraction, latched teacher gap, audit lag, work split into tools / model refit); sparkline by source; faded labelled last-DFT density; Save/Load/Forget model; session autosave with ownership; smooth motion (render-thread interpolation) with the aliasing note and a "watchable" speed preset; 16 ms tick pacing with a lead cap; Vulkan-first adapter; the GL-backend teardown crash fixed at the root.

## Honest limits

- **Valid only where taught.** The model inherits every teacher approximation (smearing, LSDA, the box) and adds its own fit error; the uncertainty shown is the model's, not nature's.
- **Box confinement** is the dominant corruption of teacher labels: an answer with face density above 2e-5·(h/0.25)² (≈ 50 Ha per e/Bohr³ at h = 0.25) is never trained on. Consequence: centred stretched H₂ beyond ≈ 4 Bohr at 49³ is never learned — honest, because the confined surface depends on orientation and no rotation-invariant model can represent it. An energy-relative rule (κ from the HOMO eigenvalue) is the next refinement.
- **Audit saturation:** at speed 1000 the background auditor runs one audit per teacher solve (≈ 2–4e4 steps); a failed audit arrives that late.
- **S = 4 species is not interactive** (145 steps/s at N = 20; 260 s first refit) — a spectrally truncated Σ and a warm-started evidence profile are the fixes.
- **Oxygen at h = 0.25** comes out a singlet ≈ half its resolved energy (smeared core); the reference follows the teacher; Hund's rule is an oracle for the pseudopotential milestone.
- The policy radii (quarantine 0.1×, thinning 0.25× of the envelope) and vote count (3) are geometric choices, documented in `references.md`.
