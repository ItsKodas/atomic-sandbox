## Global Constraints

- Rule 1: per-element data are Z and measured isotope mass only. The bond detector's constants (M = 33, ρ_floor = 1e-2 e/Bohr³, R_max = 8 Bohr, dr = h/2, the march step h/2 and its stationary-point fraction f_g = 0.25; δ = 0.10 and ε_⊥ = 0.07 provisional — ≥ 3× the ≈ 3 % / 2.2 % noise the re-review measured on the gridded fixture — re-measured in Task 3 and on the real surface in Task 7, raised if a measurement demands, never lowered) are universal; the promolecule is *solved* and shell-binned from the grid, never tabulated; names, symbols, the element ordering and the trivial-name table are display data in `app` only.
- Rule 2: hand-written everything; `rand` only for the sampling pass (unchanged).
- Rule 3: atomic units inside `sim`, `providers`, `qm-gpu`; conversions at the UI edge through `units`.
- Rule 4: f64 on the CPU (references, detector, FIRE); f32 on the grid; the density samples cross as f32 → f64.
- Rule 5: `sim` never learns what computed a density: `sample_density` returns numbers, the detector consumes numbers.
- Rule 6: every existing suite keeps its numbers through the refactor (Task 1's acceptance); new bounds are pinned in `references.md` and never loosened.
- Rule 7: the LiH and HeH⁺ oracles are *reported, not asserted* (spec §3.6: the ionic / charged closed-shell cases under a neutral promolecule); the H₃⁺, water and He₂ oracles are pre-measured before their pins are written; no charge-dependent rescaling of the promolecule; ρ_b at h = 0.25 is labelled non-physical for Z ≥ 3.
- Rule 8: FIRE's Δt is its own (`relax::Fire`), never the run's dt; `sim_time` does not advance during a relax.
- One-surface bookkeeping stays exact: every FIRE step books ΔE_KS + ΔK into W (E_conserved constant to rounding through a relax, spec P8); `SetIsotope`'s ΔK is booked like the thermostat's.
- Scene files and presets round-trip byte-identically (A written only when not the most abundant).
- Timings reported, never asserted (R3).

## Review Focus

1. **A false bond between two atoms that merely sit near a third** (the H–H of water: ρ on that chord is oxygen's). Expected: no H–H line, ever — and the O–H bonds still found although the smeared O–H chord has no interior minimum. Pinned in Task 3 `a_third_atom_does_not_bond_two_neighbours_and_the_x_h_bonds_take_the_deformation_route` (the H–H rise ends at the O nucleus; O–H through Route D) and Task 7 `water_has_two_oh_bonds_and_no_hh_bond`. Its mirror image — a real three-centre bond rejected because its transverse rise ends at a ring point — is pinned in Task 3 `a_three_centre_ring_is_kept_by_the_march` and Task 7 `h3_plus_is_a_three_centre_ring`. And the verdict must not depend on how the molecule sits in the box: `the_march_is_rotation_invariant` (Task 3) replays both fixtures at 45°, 90° and a seeded random rotation and requires identical verdicts (a straight-line march gave a false H–H bond at 45°).
2. **A closed-shell contact reported as a bond** (two He pushed together), or a verdict that flips with where the sample point sits in its grid cell. Expected: no line, even though ρ between them exceeds the floor, and the same verdict at every cell offset. Pinned in Task 3 `a_depleted_overlap_is_not_a_bond` and `the_same_operator_cancels_the_interpolation_bias` (the enhancement's spread over 24 placements ≤ 0.02 against a raw-ratio spread ≥ 0.05), and Task 7 `he2_is_a_closed_shell_contact_not_a_bond` (asserted with the measured 3× margin).
3. **Stale bonds presented as current under the surrogate**, or drawn with wrong indices after an atom is removed. Expected: faded + labelled, and dropped on a count change. Pinned in Task 3 `bonds_go_stale_on_surrogate_steps_and_drop_on_count_change` (driver test with the scripted provider).
4. **The isotope mass not reaching the integrator** (D₂ vibrating like H₂) or the scene file dropping A on save. Pinned in Task 5 `a_deuterium_dimer_vibrates_slower_on_the_morse_surface` (period ratio on the analytic dimer) and `scene_round_trips_mass_numbers_and_keeps_old_files_byte_identical`; Task 7 `d2_vibrates_slower_than_h2_by_the_mass_ratio`.
5. **A relax that shows up as drift or as a jump in E_conserved**, or that runs the clock, or that cannot converge while a nucleus is held on the spring. Pinned in Task 6 `fire_books_every_step_and_keeps_e_conserved_constant` (|ΔE_conserved| ≤ 1e-10 across the whole relax including the backtracks; W = E_start − E_end to 1e-10 at the end; `sim_time` unchanged; history restarted once at the end) and `the_stop_criterion_uses_the_augmented_force`; Task 7 `fire_relaxes_h3_plus_to_the_quenched_minimum` (|ΔE_conserved| ≤ 1e-10 on the real surface).

Rules the detector implements (spec §3.1): per pair, chord samples ρ̂_k = `pro.corrected(p_k, ρ_k)`; **Route S** when the interior minimum of ρ̂ lies below both endpoints: p* there; transverse test on ρ̂ with `EPS_TRANSVERSE`; on failure the **gradient march** (spec §3.1, R-1): first direction g⊥ = Σ_k [ρ̂(p* + h e_k) − ρ̂(p* − h e_k)] e_k from the round-2 samples; each step of h/2 then re-estimates ∇ρ̂ from the ±h/2 probes on the three axes (central differences, corrected by `pro.corrected`) and turns along it; stops, in this order: the next point within h of any nucleus → `NotATransverseMaximum`; ρ̂ decreased → `multi_centre`; |∇ρ̂| < `MARCH_STATIONARY_FRACTION` × its value at the first march step → `multi_centre`; 2·R_MAX/h steps → `NotATransverseMaximum`; **Route D** otherwise: q_k = ρ_k / `pro.trilinear(p_k)`, p* at the interior maximum of q (must exceed both endpoint values of q, else `NoInteriorMinimum`), transverse test on q (q at p* ± h e_k computed from round-2 samples with the same correction). Then `BelowFloor` (ρ̂(p*) < `RHO_FLOOR`), then `NotAccumulated` (q(p*) < 1 + `DELTA`). The report carries every number whether or not the pair is bonded.
